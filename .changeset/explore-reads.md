In a generated project, `/sdlc-mode` sends exploration to the `explore` subagent, on `haiku` unless a models rule says otherwise, in every playbook step, `playbooks/question.md` included: a read of four or more files in one step other than the files a playbook step names by path, a search whose hits the session would read, and a comparison of two directories or two branches other than the session's own diff. The session reads only the subagent's report, so the main model spends fewer tokens. With no models rule, a subagent takes the `Default effort` column of `/setup-sdlc` step 3, not an effort from that skill's step 5 example. Both rules are in `.claude/skills/sdlc-mode/SKILL.md` step 4.
