# Asking a Person

- Ask the person to do something only after a check shows that it is needed. It may already be done, or the work may not depend on it.
- When one way fails, try the other ways you have before you ask the person. A step that fails for want of a permission is the exception: stop, and say what failed and what the person can do.
- Before you ask the person a question, look for the answer in the files, the configuration, and the service. Ask the person only for a decision.
- Finish every step you can do before you reply. A reply lists what you did and the decisions that remain. It holds a step for the person only when a rule reserves that action for a person.
- When a rule reserves an action for a person, verify every precondition first and put everything into one run, so that the person runs it once.

_Completion criterion_: Every request to the person is a decision or an action a rule reserves for a person, and a check in this turn shows it is needed.
